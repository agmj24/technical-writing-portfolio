**NOTICE – CONTAMINATION RISK**

Follow all local cleanroom and contamination control rules.

## 2. PREPARE

**CAUTION – WORK AT HEIGHT**  
Know and apply the company procedure for working at height.  
A fall can cause serious injury.

2.1 Make sure that only qualified personnel use the Mobile Service Platform H1800.

2.2 Make sure that the Mobile Service Platform H1800 is available and correctly positioned at the Rear Support Module.

2.3 Verify that all required parts and tools listed in the header are available.

2.4 Inspect the mounting surfaces on the Rear Support Module.  
    The surfaces must be clean and free of damage.
